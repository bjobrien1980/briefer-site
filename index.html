BHP Port Operations bargaining
BHP · AWU / ETU / AMWU · Port Hedland
High materiality
Observed
Official ETU and WMWA posts report combined protected action by hundreds of members, including ship-loader movement bans and a 48-hour action window. WMWA separately reports an 11th bargaining meeting conducted with FWC assistance.
Assessment
The action demonstrates practical coordination across three unions and a capacity to target a high-leverage point in the iron ore chain. The available evidence establishes the union account; employer and independent operational evidence are not yet connected.
Watch for
A revised BHP proposal, further notified action, a union member update, changes to the REM/classification/allowance claims, or evidence of operational recovery and impact.
