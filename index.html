Building Cooperative Workplaces reforms

In force · 1 July 2026

Payday Super

Employers must pay superannuation contributions with salary or wages so they generally reach the nominated account within seven business days.

Pilbara relevance: payroll, enterprise-agreement and contractor compliance. The change is operational rather than evidence of an industrial issue at any named employer.
